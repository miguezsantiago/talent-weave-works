Finding the right candidate is half the job. The other half — hiring them properly, paying them on time and staying on the right side of every regulation — is where most hires get stuck, especially when the company and the talent are in different countries. That's why we've partnered with **Deel**.

## What Deel is

Deel is one of the leading global hiring and payroll platforms. It takes care of everything that comes after "yes, I want this person on my team":

- **Compliant local contracts** in more than 150 countries, with no need to open a legal entity in each one.
- **Employer of Record (EOR):** Deel acts as the talent's local employer, while the company directs the day-to-day work.
- **Contractor management:** contracts, invoicing and payments for freelancers and contractors in a single place.
- **International payroll:** payments in local currency, with withholdings and taxes taken care of.

For the Argentine market this is no minor detail: a large share of local tech talent already works for companies abroad through platforms like Deel.

## What you gain when your recruiting agency has this partnership

Until now the process came in two separate stretches: we presented the shortlist in 72 hours, you closed the candidate… and then your team had to sort out onboarding, the contract and payments on its own. With this partnership, the two stretches are connected:

1. **A direct introduction to the Deel team.** Once the candidate is closed, we connect you with Deel so onboarding starts right away, without building the commercial relationship from scratch.
2. **Hire in Argentina and LATAM without a local entity.** If your company is based abroad, you can bring on the talent we introduce without setting up a legal structure here.
3. **Preferential terms for Meiba clients.** Deals that come through our partnership get access to discounts at Deel, with terms defined case by case according to the type and volume of hiring.

The result: the time between "they accepted the offer" and "they're working and getting paid" gets shorter, and compliance risk stops being your problem.
