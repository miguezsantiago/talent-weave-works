Deciding how much to pay is one of the hardest calls when opening a tech search in Argentina. Pay too little and you attract no one; pay too much and you eat into your margin. On top of that, the market moves fast and a large part of it is benchmarked in dollars. This guide gives you a framework to build a salary band that is competitive and defensible.

## Why the "market average" isn't enough

Searching for "developer salary in Argentina" and taking the average is an expensive mistake. That number mixes juniors with seniors, Buenos Aires with the rest of the country, and local companies with ones that pay in dollars. A useful salary band is defined by **role + seniority + payment arrangement**, not by a general average.

## The three factors that define the band

1. **Real seniority, not years of experience.** What matters is which problems the person can solve autonomously, not how many years appear on the résumé.
2. **Exposure to the dollar market.** If the profile can work remotely for companies abroad, your competition pays in dollars. Ignoring that means losing the candidate before you start.
3. **Type of contract.** Full-time employment, a self-employed arrangement (monotributo) or a contractor paid in dollars completely change the net amount the person takes home.

## How to build the band in practice

| Step | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Anchor the role | Define seniority by responsibilities, not by tenure |
| 2. Gather fresh data | Use up-to-date sources for the specific role, not averages from a year ago |
| 3. Set a floor and a ceiling | The band needs a range: a floor for the minimum fit, a ceiling for the ideal candidate |
| 4. Add the full package | Bonuses, equity, vacation days and flexibility compete too |

## The mistake of negotiating without a defined band

Going to market without a settled band leads you to improvise at the offer stage, and that's where processes fall apart. A band defined from day one lets you filter for aligned candidates and **close fast** when the right one shows up. Speed, as we covered in [how long it takes to fill a tech role](/en/blog/cuanto-tarda-cubrir-vacante-tech), is part of the offer.

## Compensation is not just money

Especially in tech, the total package carries weight: remote work, autonomy, a modern stack, projects with impact and a healthy culture often tip the balance against a slightly higher offer. If your band is competitive but not the highest, lean on these differentiators.
