Hiring a warehouse operator and hiring the person who runs that operation are two different searches. The first job post often fills quickly. The second — a logistics manager, a warehouse lead or a supply chain leader — stalls: the right person is already employed, they want a clear mandate, and while the seat sits empty you feel it in delays, messy inventory and a team covering two roles. The problem is especially visible in Buenos Aires and Greater Buenos Aires (AMBA).

## Why these searches have gotten harder

Logistics has been growing as an employer. In March 2026, [Randstad Argentina](https://www.randstad.com.ar/quienes-somos/press-releases/10-perfiles-laborales-impulsados-crecimiento-industria-logistica/) noted that the sector is consolidating as one of the most dynamic in job creation, driven by e-commerce, the expansion of distribution centers and pressure to gain efficiency in the last mile. Demand is concentrated in large urban centers, and technical and professional roles are growing too.

At the same time, those people are hard to find. According to [ManpowerGroup's 2026 Talent Shortage Survey](https://mercado.com.ar/tendencias/escasez-de-talento-64-de-empleadores-en-argentina-no-logra-cubrir-puestos), 64% of employers in Argentina have difficulty filling roles. In commerce and logistics the figure is the same: 64%. There is pressure to fill the operational floor, and a shortage of people who can run a distribution center, a planning function or a distribution network.

## Define the role before you start searching

"Logistics manager" is not a single profile. Before you post anything, spell out the problem this person has to solve in the first 90 days:

- **Warehouse or operations lead:** shifts, people, safety, productivity, and a WMS that actually gets used.
- **Supply chain or planning:** demand, inventory, suppliers and service, with judgment to balance cost and service levels.
- **Distribution or transportation:** routing, in-house or third-party fleets, and the last mile.
- **Foreign trade:** customs, costs and lead times for a cross-border operation.

Also define who they report to, how many people they will manage, and what is essential versus what can be learned on the job. A mixed profile ("warehouse, customs, data, and 100% on site") stretches the search and lowers the quality of the shortlist.

## Where these searches get stuck

The best candidates at this level are almost never applying to job posts: they already run a shift or a distribution center in Greater Buenos Aires or another urban hub. If your process relies only on a job board, you are left with the minority who are actively looking.

The other usual bottlenecks are the same ones we covered in [how long it takes to fill a role](/en/blog/cuanto-tarda-cubrir-vacante-tech): a profile that changes mid-search, interviews that do not land because of calendars, and a salary band improvised at the offer stage. In logistics there is an extra requirement: combining leadership of large teams with systems (WMS, OMS, routing) and with an operation that cannot stop.

## How to speed up without lowering the bar

1. **Lock the mandate and the salary band from day one.** Without that, every "almost right" candidate reopens the internal debate.
2. **Go after passive talent.** Mapping comparable distribution centers, operators and supply chain teams is slower at the start and faster at the close.
3. **Compress the interviews.** A visit to the operation tells you more than four remote meetings. Block a slot for the walkthrough and the interview with the decision-maker.
4. **Assess leadership and the scoreboard, not just a logistics résumé.** Having "worked in logistics" is not enough: what matters is how they measured service, cost and people.
5. **Name a single decision-maker.** If operations, finance and general management all have to agree and no one closes, the search stretches on its own.

If the role is critical, the internal team does not have hours to source, or you have gone weeks without qualified candidates, [outsourcing the search](/en/blog/que-es-headhunter-cuando-tercerizar-busqueda-tech) is often cheaper than leaving the seat empty. The [cost of an open management role](/en/blog/cuanto-cuesta-vacante-gerencial-abierta) shows up quickly in an operation that runs every day.
