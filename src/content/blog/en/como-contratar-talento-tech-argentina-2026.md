Hiring tech talent in Argentina has changed. You're no longer competing only with the company next door: you're up against companies from the United States and Europe that pay in dollars and hire remotely. In this context, the selection process is no longer an HR formality. It's a competitive advantage.

This guide sums up what works today to add tech profiles without losing months or candidates.

## 1. Define the role before you start searching

The most common mistake is opening a search with a generic title ("Full-stack developer") and a list of technologies. Before you post anything, answer three questions:

- **What specific problem will this person solve in the first 90 days?**
- **What is essential, and what can be learned on the job?**
- **Who will they work with, and who do they report to?**

A well-defined profile cuts candidate review time in half.

## 2. Speed is part of the offer

In the Argentine tech market, the best profiles receive several offers at once and are usually off the market in under two weeks. If your process has five interviews spread over a month, you'll lose the best ones before you get to the offer.

Agile processes that work:

1. Initial screening (30 min).
2. A focused, concrete technical interview.
3. Team interview / culture fit.
4. Offer.

Three well-designed stages tell you more than six poorly planned ones.

## 3. Pay for the market, not for the pay scale

Tech salaries in Argentina move with real demand and are often benchmarked in dollars. Before you define a salary band, look at up-to-date data for the specific role and seniority, not general averages. A misaligned offer means wasting time on candidates who were never going to accept.

## 4. Candidate experience matters

Every touchpoint communicates what it's like to work with you: how clear the job post is, how fast you respond, the feedback after each stage. A candidate who doesn't move forward but leaves with a good impression is a future lead or referral.

## 5. When a headhunter makes sense

If the role is critical, scarce or strategic — or if your team doesn't have the hours to dedicate to sourcing — a specialized headhunter speeds up the process and gives you access to passive candidates you would never see on a job board.
